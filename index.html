Using Pivot

Try these steps with the sample mark or upload an SVG of your own.

  1. Shape the logo. Upload an SVG with text converted to paths. In Shape, adjust the extrusion depth, bevel, and size.
  2. Choose a material. Pick a finish under Material. For ambientCG, unzip one pack and select its maps together under Custom textures. Save a finish to your material library to reuse it in this browser. Grass Blades has length and density controls.
  3. Light and frame it. In Scene, choose lighting, adjust reflections and fill, and set a square or vertical frame. Choose a background color, background image, or transparency.
  4. Make a loop. In Motion, try Spin, Rock, or Light Sweep. Choose an axis, direction, and duration. Click Export and choose MP4 or WebM.
  5. Make a still. Click Pose for PNG. Drag to turn or tilt the logo, or use the three axis sliders. Export a PNG with or without the background. Back to video resumes from that pose.
